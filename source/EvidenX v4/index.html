Frequently Asked Questions

Is EvidenX free to use?
Yes, EvidenX is currently 100% free for individuals. You can detect AI-generated videos, images, audio, and text without any cost.
Is my data private and secure?
Absolutely. We use local processing technology, meaning your files (photos, videos, audio) are analyzed directly in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers.
Do I need to sign up?
No. EvidenX requires no account, no login, and no credit card. Just open the page and start verifying.
Can it detect Photoshoped Images?
Yes. Our multi-modal engine supports Image verification(Using Heatmaps,etc) to identify Edited/Altered Images edited by Softwares like Photoshop,Snapseed,etc.
